Checking the dice

After a match, open the dice log (the shield at the top of the game) and tap “Copy for checking elsewhere”. The fingerprint is SHA-256 of the seed (8 bytes, big-endian) followed by the 32-byte salt; every roll is generated from the seed with SplitMix64.
